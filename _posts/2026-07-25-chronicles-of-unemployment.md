I resigned from my second job where I was there for 3+ years and was unemployed for a period of 3 months. Here is some bits and pieces of my experience. 
Will keep this updated as I relive those moments.

---
Ecstasy that comes right after leaving a job you wanted to leave for a while is one of the great pleasures of life.

---
The Crash that comes after crashes you to the deepest part. You will be miserable and heart will be racing in 
waves like the tides on full moon.

---
Even if you are not financially vulnerable, 
your mind still thinks you are in danger. It wants to get a job ASAP. But irrational. 

---
You endup applying for jobs you would have never applied low money, rude interviewers, abnormal office timings all for going back to the "normal".

---
You get emotionally tired after giving so many interviews. 
You don't really want to apply for jobs. Let them come themselves, you say.

---
Few Recruiters will be rude but most don't care about resignation or a small career gap.
